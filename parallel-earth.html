Parallel Earth // In Development

One world.
Endless consequences.

Parallel Earth is a decision-driven world simulation where your choices influence the future of humanity.

SimulationInteractive StoryStrategy
The premise

The world is changing. You decide how it responds.

Artificial intelligence, climate pressure, resource shortages, biotechnology, international conflict, and discoveries beyond Earth can all reshape the future. Each scenario forces a decision, and each decision creates consequences.

01 // DECIDE

Make the Call

Choose how Earth responds when there is no perfect answer.

02 // CONSEQUENCE

See What Changes

Watch the world state shift based on the path you choose.

03 // EVOLVE

Shape a Timeline

Build a version of Earth defined by your decisions over time.
