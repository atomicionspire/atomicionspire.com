Privacy Policy for Parallel Earth

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Atomic Ionspire Studios respects your privacy. Parallel Earth does not require an account, login, name, email address, phone number, or other information that directly identifies you. Game decisions and world state are processed locally by the app unless a feature described below requires a third-party service.

Information Collected by Parallel Earth

Parallel Earth itself does not currently ask users to submit personal information and does not operate its own user account database. Core game state is processed locally in the app.

Advertising and Google AdMob

Parallel Earth may display advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When advertising is enabled, Google may automatically collect or process certain information in order to deliver ads, measure performance, provide analytics, and prevent fraud or abuse. Depending on device settings, region, consent choices, and SDK configuration, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device or account identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available;
  • app interactions such as app launches, taps, ad views, and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information such as crash, hang, launch-time, or energy-use data; and
  • advertising-related information used to serve, measure, or protect ads.

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for Android is encrypted in transit. You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at Google's partner sites and apps information page and review Google's privacy policy at Google Privacy Policy.

Personalized and Non-Personalized Ads

Depending on applicable law, your region, age-related requirements, device settings, and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Where required, consent choices may be presented before advertising technologies are used for personalized advertising.

Data Sharing

Atomic Ionspire Studios does not sell personal information. Information processed through Google AdMob may be shared with Google and its advertising partners as necessary to provide advertising, analytics, security, and fraud-prevention services, subject to Google's policies and applicable law.

Children's Privacy

Parallel Earth does not ask children or other users to provide personal information directly. If the app is made available to audiences that include children, Atomic Ionspire Studios will configure advertising and consent settings as required by applicable law and platform policies. Parents or guardians with privacy concerns may contact Atomic Ionspire Studios through this website.

Data Retention

Parallel Earth does not currently maintain its own account database or cloud storage of player profiles. Data handled by Google AdMob or other third-party services is retained according to those providers' own policies and legal obligations.

Your Choices

You may be able to control advertising identifiers, ad personalization, and privacy settings through your device or Google account settings. Where legally required, the app may also provide consent or privacy controls related to advertising.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when Parallel Earth adds, removes, or changes features, advertising, analytics, subscriptions, or other services. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Atomic Ionspire Studios through atomicionspire.com.
